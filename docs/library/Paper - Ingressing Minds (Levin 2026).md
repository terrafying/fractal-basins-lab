# Paper — Ingressing Minds: Causal, Non-Physical Patterns In-Form Natural, Synthetic, and Hybrid Embodiments

Minds pre-exist as patterns in a Platonic space; bodies are interfaces through
which patterns ingress. Vocabulary descends from Whitehead (ingression,
eternal objects). Nested cognitive light cones; collective intelligence of
parts; minds as superset of life.

## Relation + cautions
