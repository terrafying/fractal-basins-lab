# Paper — TAME (Levin 2022)

Technological Approach to Mind Everywhere. Cognition is scale-free and
continuous (not binary); bodies are collective intelligences of parts with
their own agendas; minds are not generated by brains but are collective
dynamics that can ingress into many substrates. Introduces/uses
[[Concept - cognitive light cone]] — the set of goals/states an agent can
represent and pursue; its size defines the agent's cognitive scale.

## Hook for our program
The basin = the model's light cone, operationalized. GLM-5.3's objection
(agent goal-horizon vs observer-bookkept outcome-coincidence) means we should
frame it as a MEASURED ANALOGUE, not an operationalization. TAME's
"persuadability continuum" maps to our intervention question: can the atlas
be re-sculpted by touching parts (saddle-kick)?
