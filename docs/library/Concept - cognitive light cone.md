# Concept — cognitive light cone

Levin (Computational Boundary of a Self, 2019): the span of goals and future
states an agent can represent and pursue. Selves are computational boundaries
around collectives of sub-agents; light cones nest across scales (cells <>
tissues <> organisms).

## Our operationalization (measured analogue)
The basin: the set of initial conditions whose futures coincide (same final
answer, same settling class). Size, boundary dimension, and interlocking are
all measurable. GLM-5.3's caveat: Levin's cone is the AGENT's goal-horizon
(indistinguishability-to-itself, including counterfactual futures); our basin
is outcome-coincidence bookkept by the observer. Frame as measured analogue.
Exp09d's layer stack = light-cone cross-sections at different computation
depths.
