# Paper — Language Models Learn Universal Representations of Numbers (2025)

Reviews the number-encoding literature: Nanda 2023 (modular addition clock),
Kantamneni & Tegmark 2025 (helix), Kadlcik 2025 (accurate linear number
representations), Levy & Geva 2025 (digit-wise base-10 encoding), Zhu 2025
(linear value encoding), Golkar 2023 (XVal continuous encoding).

## Question it answers for us
Our number-manifold chart (exp09c) assumed structure exists. This paper
surveys the competing encoding hypotheses (helix vs linear vs digit-wise) —
our chart methodology (steer along PCA of number-token embeddings, read the
answer) could DISCRIMINATE between them: helix predicts periodic bands,
linear predicts monotone gradient, digit-wise predicts base-10 digit structure.
