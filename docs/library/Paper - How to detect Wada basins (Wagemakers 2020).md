# Paper — How to detect Wada basins (Wagemakers 2020)

Review of detection techniques: merge method (magnification test), grid
search, boundary box-counting, converts-to-boundary tests. Also covers
partial/almost Wada and uncertainty exponents.

## Use for us
The menu of rigorous tests to replace our crude triple-junction proxy.
Also the notion of "almost Wada" (fraction of boundary shared) matches what
we actually want to claim: what FRACTION of settling-boundary points are
shared by multiple settling basins.
