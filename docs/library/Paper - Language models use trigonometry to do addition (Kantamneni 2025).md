# Paper — Language models use trigonometry to do addition (Kantamneni 2025)

Numbers in LLMs are represented as a generalized helix in activation space;
addition is performed by rotating along the helix (the "Clock" algorithm).
Causally implicated for add/subtract, relevant for multiply/divide/mod.

## Direct hook
Our exp09c number chart used digit-PCA directions and found diagonal bands.
This paper says the right basis is the helix: a circular Fourier pair plus a
linear monotone component. Re-charting the activation plane in helical
coordinates should turn our diagonal bands into clean concentric structure —
and give a rigorous test: is the band periodicity the helix period?
