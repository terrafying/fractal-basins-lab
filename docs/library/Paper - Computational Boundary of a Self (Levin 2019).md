# Paper — The Computational Boundary of a Self (Levin 2019)

Defines the cognitive light cone: the span of goals and states an agent can
represent and pursue. Selves are computational boundaries around collectives
of sub-agents. Nested light cones across scales.

## Relation
Our settling-basin maps are candidate cross-sections of a model's light cone
at different depths (exp09d stack). The scaling question (does the cone grow
with capability?) is our model-family sweep.
